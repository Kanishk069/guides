# RESEARCH NOTES (guide mein jo cheezein inse aayi)

*Dates, thresholds aur clocks sab config tables mein rakhna. Hardcode nahi, aur legal/finance team se verify karwana.*

**Strategy execution:**
- OKR software goal setting aur tracking karta hai, jabki strategy execution software un goals ko portfolio, funding aur value realization se jodta hai. Strategy mapping, scenario/what-if analysis, financial governance aur resource/capacity management enterprise platforms ke core capabilities hain.
- Annual strategic goal se quarterly OKR aur phir team ke key result tak cascade hi alignment gap band karta hai.

**M&A aur deal governance:**
- Data rooms ab akele nahi rahe, integrated deal platforms ban rahe hain jo document management ko project management aur post-merger integration planning se jodte hain. VDR mein granular permissions, documents se linked due-diligence checklists, aur Q&A workflow (assignment, approval, status) hona chahiye. Due diligence aam taur par 30 se 90 din leti hai.
- **India merger control:** Asset, turnover ya ₹2,000 crore deal-value threshold cross karne wala combination CCI ko notify karna padta hai aur approval se pehle complete nahi ho sakta. Review clock 150 din ka hai aur "material influence" control test codified hai. Deal value threshold tab lagta hai jab transaction value INR 20 billion se upar ho aur target ke India mein substantial business operations hon. Deal value mein har direct/indirect, deferred ya non-cash consideration aata hai. Interconnected transactions ke liye approval ek saath leni padti hai.

**Sustainability strategy:**
- CSRD Omnibus ke baad bhi double materiality mandatory hai. Omnibus se in-scope companies ki sankhya bahut kam hui (lagbhag 80-90%). Directive (EU) 2026/470 18 March 2026 se force mein hai, aur India mein SEBI BRSR top 1,000 listed companies ke liye mandatory hai, BRSR Core assurance phase-wise badh raha hai. ISSB financial materiality par focus karta hai, CSRD double materiality par.

**Customer experience / single view:**
- Identity resolution mein deterministic aur probabilistic dono matching chalti hain, consent ek simple boolean nahi balki multi-dimensional state hai, aur ek insaan ke kai identifiers hone se data-subject rights ke workflows linked identifiers par chalne padte hain. Single Customer View sirf merged table nahi, governed data product hai, aur identity resolution ek continuous operation hai, one-time project nahi. Consent withdraw hone par change connected sab platforms tak ek hi action mein propagate hona chahiye.

**Org design:**
- Org modeling mein positions drag-drop karke headcount, FTE, cost, span aur layers ka live impact dikhta hai, scenarios side-by-side compare hote hain, aur finalize hone par approval route hota hai. Scenario ko baseline se compare karna aur reporting lines badalte hi spans/layers auto-recalculate hona zaruri hai. Position-level cost, vacancy modeling aur sensitive-access controls requirements mein aate hain. Roles eliminate/consolidate/relocate karne ke internal mobility aur retention par asar bhi model karne padte hain, sirf span-layer-cost kaafi nahi.

**Competitive intelligence:**
- CI tools competitor tracking, battlecards, win-loss analysis aur market monitoring automate karte hain. Corporate strategy (M&A, market entry, portfolio planning) aur strategic foresight/scenario planning alag use cases hain.

- **Strategy-as-graph:** vision to mission to option to strategy to objective to initiative to measure, sab `strategy_nodes` + `strategy_links` se. Koi bhi objective ya initiative jo strategy se linked nahi, orphan flag hota hai.
- **Versioning + baseline:** har approved strategy artefact versioned, "as-of date" se koi bhi report.
- **Evidence and assumptions:** har strategic decision ke saath evidence aur assumptions. Assumption `invalid` hone par linked nodes `at_risk` flag hote hain aur owner ko review task jata hai.

- **Approval engine:** parallel steps, quorum, board-level approvals, delegation, SLA escalation.

**Dependencies (naam se):** Approval Engine, Notification Engine, Document Store/Data Room, Finance/ERP/FP&A, HR Core, Product Portfolio (2.0), CRM/CDP, Compliance/Legal, Analytics/BI, PPM/Jira.

### Core Logic
1. Business model adopt tabhi ho sakta hai jab approvals complete aur integration points ke owners ne sign-off kiya ho. Adoption `effective_from` se (effective-dated), purana model retire/update.
2. Integration conflict (shared resource, cannibalization) quantified aur Module 6 targets ko adjust karne ka suggestion.
3. Maintenance triggers auto: KPI breach ya assumption invalid hone par `bm_feedback` auto-create.
4. Feedback priority score = impact x urgency, accepted feedback se update version, purana version history mein.
5. Governance: periodic attestation due date par owner ko reminder, miss hone par council ko escalation.
6. Communication: restricted/MNPI content disclosure review (legal/IR) ke bina send nahi, quiet period mein external messages block.
7. Strategy/vision/mission versions publish hone par comm plan auto-suggest, audience segments HR/CRM se dynamic.
8. Read/acknowledge tracking aur non-acknowledgers ko reminders, aur understanding survey effectiveness metrics mein.
9. Multi-language messages `translations` se, country-specific legal edits ke saath.
10. `audit_log` har action pe.
