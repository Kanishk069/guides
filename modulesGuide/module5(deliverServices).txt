Theek hai bhai, pehle research karta hun, phir poori guide ek saath dunga.Research ho gayi. Jo cheezein guide mein daali hain, unka nichod:

- PSA platforms project management, resource planning, time/expense, billing aur reporting ko ek system mein jodte hain. Isliye Module 3, 4, 7, 8 aur 9 ek hi data model par bane hain.
- ITIL 4 mein SLA customer, internal team aur third party, teeno ke beech lagu hota hai. OLA internal team ka woh agreement hai jo SLA ko support karta hai.
- "Watermelon effect" matlab SLA green par customer naraz. Isse bachne ke liye Module 1 mein XLA aur health score hai.
- ASC 606 / IFRS 15 mein T&M, fixed-price, milestone aur retainer ka recognition alag hota hai. Jo fixed-fee milestones completion se match nahi karte, unke liye percentage-completion method chahiye. Isliye Module 9 mein multi-method revenue recognition hai.
- Forecast mein accuracy ke saath bias aur Forecast Value Added (FVA) bhi track hota hai. Isse pata chalta hai ki statistical, planner ya executive, kis step ne forecast sudhara ya bigaada. Accuracy lag-wise bhi naapi jati hai.
- Skills ke liye taxonomy aur ontology dono ek saath chahiye. Skills kitni ek doosre ki jagah le sakti hain (fungibility), yeh capacity planning ke liye zaroori hai.
- Feedback mein CSAT aur CES transactional hote hain, NPS relationship wala. Closed-loop mein low score par customer ko follow-up jata hai. Health score in teeno ko milakar banta hai.

**Common rules:** API prefix `/api/sd/...`. Har create/edit/action `audit_log` mein jayega. Har master `effective_from/effective_to` aur `status[draft/active/disabled]` ke saath hoga (delete nahi, disable). Har table mein `entity_id` aur `tenant_id` hoga (multi-company, multi-tenant). **(NEW) Enterprise common rules:**
- **Idempotency:** har POST action API `Idempotency-Key` header leti hai, taaki double-click ya retry se duplicate record na bane. Har table mein `version` (optimistic locking) hoga.
- **Event-driven:** har state change `domain_events` (outbox pattern) mein jayega. Dusre modules, webhooks aur integrations wahin se subscribe karenge.

- **AI-assist rule:** har AI suggestion `ai_suggestions` mein log hoga (model, version, input hash, output). Human accept ya reject karega. Auto-apply sirf config se aur sirf low-risk actions par.
- **Data classification:** har table/field par tag (public/internal/confidential/restricted). Masking aur row-level access ke hooks abhi se rakho. Security layer baad mein plug hogi.
- **Customer isolation:** customer-confidential data par `customer_id` scope. Ek customer ka data dusre customer ke project ya report mein kabhi nahi dikhega.
- **Bulk + API:** har master ke liye bulk import/export, webhooks, aur rate-limit hooks.

### Core Logic
1. Har service ek owner aur kam se kam ek active agreement/target se linked hoga. Bina owner ya agreement ke service `live` nahi hogi.
2. RACI versioned hai. Publish par purana version `effective_to` ho jata hai aur Approval Engine chalta hai.
3. SLA measurement business calendar se hoti hai (pause/resume rules, exclusions apply). Period close par `sd_sla_measurements` ban jati hain.
4. SLA breach par credit rule se `credit_amount` calc hota hai aur Finance ko credit-note proposal jata hai (Module 9 financial close).
5. OLA/UC miss hone par uske upar ke SLA ko "at_risk" flag milta hai (dependency chain se).
6. KPI RAG config thresholds se hoga, hardcode nahi. KPI values Module 6 (operations) se auto-pull ho sakti hain.
7. Error budget khatam hone par config ke hisaab se Change freeze flag lagta hai (Module 6 Tab 4 se link).
8. Customer health score = weighted sum of dimensions (`sd_health_score_config`). Score red hone par account owner ko alert aur QBR agenda mein auto-item.
9. Watermelon check: service ke saare SLAs green hon lekin health ya CSAT red ho to account flag hota hai.
10. Survey trigger event se chalta hai (ticket closed, milestone accepted, project complete). Fatigue rule (max N per contact per period) pehle check hota hai.
11. Closed-loop: score threshold se neeche aate hi `sd_feedback_cases` auto-create, callback SLA ke saath. Case band hone par outcome mandatory.
12. Report pack publish karne se pehle approval. Customer-facing pack mein internal margin/cost fields kabhi nahi jayenge (field-level class check).
13. Har change `audit_log` mein.

### Core Logic
1. Weighted pipeline demand = value/hours x calibrated probability, `staffing_templates` se role-wise lines. Hardbook (signed) alag, softbook (pipeline) alag dikhte hain.
2. Naive baseline aur statistical forecast hamesha save hote hain, taaki FVA nikal sake.
3. Consensus version = statistical + approved overrides. Har override ka reason mandatory, aur lock date ke baad override block.
4. FVA = naive error - step error. Negative FVA lagatar aaye to owner ko review flag.
5. Accuracy metrics: WAPE = sum(|actual - forecast|) / sum(actual). Bias = sum(forecast - actual) / sum(actual). Dono dikhte hain, kyunki absolute error bias chhupa deta hai.
6. Accuracy horizon/lag-wise store hota hai (jis lag par forecast liya tha, usi lag ke forecast se compare).
7. Customer forecast aur contract commitment mismatch par alert. Variance volume-band se bahar ho to commercial team ko notification.
8. Feasibility check: demand skills vs supply (booked + available + fungible substitutes), constraints (clearance, language, location). Output earliest start date + shortfall + recommended action.
9. Actual vs forecast variance threshold cross karne par auto re-forecast trigger (config) aur revision `sd_forecast_revisions` mein.
10. Model change/approval versioned hai. Naya model champion banne se pehle backtest mandatory.
11. Published consensus Module 4 (resource plan) ko feed karta hai, aur HR WFP (HR Module 2) ko long-horizon input deta hai.
12. Har forecast, override aur model run `audit_log` mein.

### Core Logic
1. Staffing requirement workload forecast (Module 3 consensus + intraday pattern) se nikalta hai: required staff = f(volume, AHT, service-level target, shrinkage). Roster us par optimize hota hai aur labor rules (Module 2) enforce hote hain.

4. SLA timer agreement target se start hota hai, business calendar se chalta hai. Pause/resume sirf allowed reasons (jaise "waiting on customer") par, aur har pause logged.
5. Timer 75%/90% par at-risk alert, 100% par breach, aur Module 1 ka `sd_sla_measurements` auto-update.
6. Severity matrix se major incident auto-suggest hota hai. Major incident declare hone par war room, comms template, status page event, aur escalation matrix ek saath chalte hain.
7. Problem tabhi close hota hai jab root cause aur known error/permanent fix link ho. Repeat incidents par problem auto-suggest.
8. Change risk score = f(service criticality, change type, past failure rate, window, dependency count). Collision detection same CI/service ke overlapping changes par.
9. Freeze calendar ya error budget exhausted ho to normal changes block, emergency change sirf CAB-emergency approval se.
10. AI triage sirf suggestion hai (`ai_suggestions`). Auto-apply tabhi jab confidence threshold aur low-risk category config mein allowed ho.
11. QA sampling rule config se (per agent, per queue, new-joiner pe zyada). Calibration sessions mein reviewers ka score variance track hota hai.
12. Audit finding par CAPA mandatory, aur effectiveness check ke baad hi band.
13. DR test due date se pehle reminder. Failed test par finding auto-create.
14. Har work item event aur change `audit_log` / `sd_work_item_events` mein.

### Core Logic

### Core Logic
1. Hard close tabhi hoga jab financial checklist (WIP zero ya approved write-off, final invoice, rev-rec true-up, cost reconciliation), acceptance, resource release aur handover sab complete hon.
2. Revenue recognition `sd_performance_obligations.recognition_method` se chalta hai. T&M approved hours x rate se, fixed-fee percentage-completion approved progress (Module 8) se, milestone acceptance par, retainer service period mein ratable, point-in-time delivery/acceptance par.
3. Fixed-fee milestone jahan billing schedule completion se match nahi karti, wahan billing-based nahi balki progress-based (percentage-completion) recognition lagega. Rule engine yeh check karke warning deta hai.
4. Billing aur revenue alag track hote hain. Contract asset = earned not billed, contract liability = billed not earned (`sd_wip_balances`).
5. Variable consideration (SLA credits, penalties, bonus) sirf tab include jab reversal ka risk config threshold se kam ho (constraint). SLA credits Module 1 ke breach se aate hain.
6. Multi-book: ek hi project ke liye US GAAP, IFRS aur management book alag schedules ban sakte hain. Revenue run period lock ke baad immutable, correction sirf reversal entry se.
7. Acceptance: contract mein deemed-acceptance days ho to us din ke baad auto-`deemed` (customer ko pehle reminder). Rejection par dispute workflow, aur milestone revenue tab tak hold.
8. Har contract obligation ka closure evidence ke saath hona chahiye. Bina closure ke warranty start nahi hoti.
9. Resource release par offboarding tasks auto-generate: IAM revoke, asset return, timesheet lock, supplier offboard, HR ko performance feedback aur verified skills/assignment history (HR employee_assignments).
10. Handover pack support team accept kare tabhi project operations (Module 6) mein transition hota hai, aur service/agreement/CMDB entries ban jati hain.
11. Knowledge asset reuse se pehle customer-confidential redaction check mandatory. Fail par asset sirf us customer ke scope mein.
12. Estimate vs actual `sd_estimate_actuals` mein jata hai aur Module 7 ki estimating ko calibrate karta hai.
13. Archive retention policy country + contract type + record type se. Legal hold active ho to delete/anonymize block. Retention khatam par disposal action customer data-return/destroy certificate ke saath.
14. System-sync tasks fail hon to retry aur alert. Koi bhi pending sync hone par project "hard close" nahi.
15. Har financial close, rev-rec run aur archive action `audit_log` mein.
