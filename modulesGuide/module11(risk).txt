Theek hai, pehle research karta hu (enterprise GRC standards aur top platforms), phir poori guide banaunga.# Research se kya nikla (guide isi ke hisaab se bani hai)

- Top GRC platforms (ServiceNow IRM, Archer, MetricStream, OneTrust) mein ek central risk register hota hai jisme poore enterprise mein consistent scoring hoti hai, multi-framework support (SOX, SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR), automated evidence collection, continuous control monitoring aur ERP/ITSM/HR/legal systems se integrations.
- Risk side pe KRI dashboards configurable thresholds aur automatic escalation ke saath, plus scenario analysis aur stress testing standard hain, aur risk ko financial terms mein quantify karna bhi.
- Regulatory side pe tools obligations inventory banate hain, horizon scanning karte hain, aur rule change ka asar seedha obligations pe nikalte hain. Obligations ko policies aur controls se map karte hain, aur har update aur decision auditable format mein record hota hai.
- Resilience ab sirf BCP nahi hai. Important business services pehle identify hote hain, impact tolerance set hoti hai, dependencies map hoti hain aur scenarios se test hota hai. DORA mein BIA, continuity plans aur regular testing required hai.
- Internal audit ke liye IIA ke Global Internal Audit Standards 9 Jan 2025 se effective hain, isliye audit module inhi pe banega.

Guide ka structure tere HR guide jaisa hi hai: Common Foundation, phir 5 Modules (1 Module = 1 Screen), har ek mein Frontend tabs, Tables, APIs, Core Logic. **Total 5 Screens / 22 Tabs**, aur har APQC number kisi tab se map hota hai (last mein coverage matrix hai).

### Core Logic
1. Appetite statement approve hone par hi active hota hai, naya version aane par purana `effective_to` ho jata hai.
2. Har tolerance limit ka `measure_source` (KRI, Ledger, ITSM, HRIS) config hota hai, aur actual value auto-pull hoti hai.
3. Cascading rule: child limit parent limit se zyada loose nahi ho sakti (validation).
4. Appetite breach hone par Risk Owner, CRO aur escalation matrix ke role ko notification, aur Module 2 mein auto-issue.
5. Policy publish hone ke baad version immutable hai, edit par naya version, aur applicable users ko acknowledgement request jati hai.
6. Escalation aur acceptance rules scoring model se linked hain. Score badalne par nayi routing auto-lagu hoti hai.
7. Lessons learned: issue/incident close hone par knowledge item ka draft auto-ban jata hai aur risk team approve karti hai.
8. Board pack generate hote waqt data ka snapshot lock ho jata hai (baad mein data badle to bhi pack nahi badlega), narrative edit hoti hai, approve hone ke baad hi publish.
9. Board ke directions `board_actions` mein jaate hain aur agle pack mein status ke saath auto-aate hain.
10. Crosswalk se pata chalta hai COSO/ISO 31000 ka kaunsa clause covered hai aur kaunsa gap hai.
11. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic
1. Risk ek hi structure mein banta hai (cause, event, impact), taki duplicate aur vague risks kam hon. Similar risk par duplicate warning.
2. Residual score = inherent score aur linked controls ki tested effectiveness (Module 3) se auto-suggest hota hai. Manual override ke liye reason mandatory hai.
3. Line 1 assess karta hai, Line 2 challenge karta hai. Challenge ke baad hi score "final" hota hai.
4. Score `risk_escalation_rules` ke range mein aaye to turant Module 1 ke escalation role ko notification aur SLA timer.
5. Risk decision "accept" to acceptance workflow, approver `risk_acceptance_rules` se. Expiry par auto re-review task.
6. Quantification: P50/P90 loss simulation se aata hai (restricted safe-eval), aur result tolerance limit se compare hota hai.
7. Insurance gap = exposure - covered limit (deductible adjust). Gap positive ho to risk par "insurance inadequate" flag, aur renewal se 90 din pehle reminder.
8. Mitigation tasks ke liye evidence upload zaruri hai. Line 2 verify kare tabhi task "verified" hota hai. Plans ka status Tab 4 ki verification queue mein aata hai.
9. KRI value RAG thresholds se auto, aur red par linked risk ka re-assessment trigger, owner ko alert. Predictive alert: trend threshold cross karne wala ho to pehle warning.
10. Incident/control-test-failure aane par linked risk par "re-assess" flag lagta hai (ITSM/Module 3 se).
11. BU adherence auto-calc: assessment done %, reporting on-time, plans on-time. Kam hone par BU head ko escalation aur Tab 5 mein RAG.
12. Roll-up: BU risks se enterprise risk par aggregate, aur same risk category ki concentration alag dikhti hai. As-of date se purani date ka view bhi.
13. Quarterly attestation na ho to BU head + CRO ko escalation.
14. Loss event close hone par root cause se Module 1 mein lessons-learned draft, aur linked risk ki likelihood re-assess.
15. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic
1. Compliance policy publish ke baad immutable hai, edit par naya version. Policy se linked obligations aur controls `grc_links` mein rehte hain, aur policy badalne par linked controls ke owners ko review notification.
2. Attestation: quiz fail par attestation complete nahi, retry. Overdue par manager aur compliance officer ko escalation. Har attestation ka timestamp, IP aur version evidence ke roop mein save hota hai.
3. Ek control multiple frameworks ke requirements ko satisfy kar sakta hai (`control_framework_map`). Ek test karo to sab frameworks mein result dikhta hai (test once, comply many).
4. Control test result `ineffective` aaye to auto-issue (`grc_issues`) banta hai, linked risks ka residual score re-assess hota hai (Module 2), aur linked obligation ka compliance status "at risk" hota hai (Module 4).
5. Testing ke teen layers hain: Line 1 CSA, Line 2 testing aur Line 3 audit. Dashboard pe teeno dikhte hain aur conflict hone par flag.
6. Whistleblower/speak-up case: anonymous reporter ke saath secure two-way messaging, case sirf assigned investigators ko dikhta hai, aur reporter ko retaliation-protection reminders. Case outcome se Module 5 mein remediation case ban sakta hai.
7. Audit universe risk-ranked hai. Audit plan mein high-risk entities zyada frequency se aate hain, aur plan committee approval se lock hota hai.
8. Auditor ko engagement lene se pehle independence declaration dena zaruri hai, aur conflict (pichle 12 mahine mein us area mein kaam) par warning.
9. Findings 4-element format mein hote hain. Management response aur action plan ke bina final report issue nahi hoti. Finding rating se closure SLA tay hota hai, aur finding `grc_issues` mein sync hoti hai.
10. Follow-up validation Internal Audit karta hai. Management ke "closed" mark karne se finding close nahi hoti, validation ke baad hi.
11. Continuous auditing jobs schedule se chalte hain, exceptions se auto-finding ya risk-signal banta hai.
12. Automated control job fail ya error aaye to control "unverified" ho jata hai, owner ko alert, aur tool health mein flag.
13. Tool ya feed stale ho (config ghante) to us par depend karne wale controls par warning.
14. Audit standards conformance checklist se gaps ka action plan banta hai aur audit committee pack mein jata hai.
15. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic
1. Har obligation ek regulation clause se linked hota hai (source traceability). Clause text badalne par linked obligations "under review" ho jaate hain.
2. Applicability: `entity_regulatory_profiles` (country, license, industry, data types) se rules chalte hain aur applicable obligations auto-suggest hoti hain. Final applicability compliance officer approve karta hai, aur "not applicable" ka rationale mandatory hai.
3. Multi-jurisdiction overlap: same requirement ke multiple obligations ek master obligation mein merge hote hain, history ke saath, taaki ek control kai laws ko cover kare.
4. Change feed se naya item aane par triage hota hai (relevant/ignore). Relevant par `reg_change_case` banta hai aur impacted obligations, policies aur controls auto-suggest hote hain (obligation_map se).
5. Change case ke stages mein SLA hota hai. Effective date se pehle action plan complete na ho to red flag aur escalation. Har decision `decision_log` aur `audit_log` mein.
6. Compliance status ka source: obligation se mapped controls ke latest test results (Module 3) plus gaps. Koi mapped control ineffective ho to obligation auto "partial/non-compliant" ho jata hai.
7. Obligation ka koi control map nahi hai to coverage matrix mein red, aur auto-gap banta hai.
8. Gap close karne ke liye remediation type chunna zaruri hai. New control banane par wo Module 3 ki control library mein jata hai, aur new policy par policy workflow chalta hai.
9. Test plan risk-based hai. `severity_if_breached` aur last result se frequency auto-suggest hoti hai. Fail test par retest plan.
10. Breach register: reportable breach par `notify_deadline` (country config se, e.g. 72 hrs) ka countdown, aur miss hone par CCO/Legal ko escalation.
11. Returns tracker mein preparer, reviewer maker-checker alag hote hain, aur submission ka acknowledgement save hota hai.
12. Sub-certification na bhare to BU head ko reminder aur escalation, exception wali certification par compliance review.
13. Scorecard weights methodology se config hote hain. Score = weighted (compliance %, test pass rate, gap ageing, overdue actions). Snapshot lock hota hai.
14. Regulatory report approve hone ke baad hi submit hota hai aur evidence bundle (jo documents/tests report ko support karte hain) saath export hota hai.
15. Regulator commitments ke due date se pehle reminder, aur miss hone par issue banta hai.
16. Inspection ke dauraan har document request ka owner aur SLA hota hai, aur findings se remediation case (Module 5) ban jata hai.
17. `audit_log` har action pe, aur regulator ko diya gaya kuch bhi `audit_log` mein read-only history ke saath rehta hai.

### Core Logic
1. Remediation case teen trigger se auto-create ho sakta hai: critical audit finding, non-compliant obligation/breach (Module 4), aur appetite breach ya critical incident (Module 1/2). Manual bhi ban sakta hai.
2. Plan ke bina case "executing" mein nahi jata. Plan approve hone par tasks active hote hain, aur dependencies (task B, task A ke baad) enforce hoti hain.
3. Dedicated resource assign karne se pehle availability aur conflict check hota hai (dusre case mein overbooked to warning), aur budget threshold ke upar approval.
4. Case ka `regulator_deadline` hone par saare tasks us tak back-planned dikhte hain, aur deadline se pehle red alert.
5. Expert engagement se pehle conflict check zaruri hai (expert ka kisi party/vendor se conflict), aur engagement letter document attach ke bina "active" nahi.
6. Legal investigation privileged hota hai. `is_privileged` records sirf Legal lead aur named users ko dikhte hain, export/print block, aur har access `audit_log` mein.
7. Legal hold issue hone par custodians ko notice jata hai, acknowledgement track hota hai, aur pending par Legal ko escalation. Hold release sirf Legal approval se.
8. Damage cause: root cause `grc_taxonomy` se chuna jata hai. Same root cause 3 se zyada cases mein aaye (config) to "systemic" flag aur Module 1 ko risk-signal.
9. Policy change proposal accept hone par Module 1/3 ke policy workflow mein draft ban jata hai, aur control change par Module 3 control library update task.
10. Case close sirf tab jab: saari tasks done, evidence attached, independent validation (Line 2 ya Internal Audit), aur lessons learned submit. Close par linked issue/gap/finding bhi close hota hai.
11. Important business service ka `impact tolerance` aur BIA ke RTO/RPO consistent hone chahiye, aur inconsistent ho to validation warning.
12. BIA ke dependencies se dependency map auto-banta hai. Ek hi dependency (vendor/app/site) kai critical services ke liye ho to SPOF flag aur Module 2 mein risk-suggestion.
13. Continuity plan har critical service par zaruri hai. Service ka plan na ho to coverage red. Plan approve hone ke baad version immutable, aur offline export (print-ready) milta hai.
14. Exercise ka result actual RTO/RPO ko BIA target se compare karta hai. Target miss hone par auto-issue, retest schedule, aur plan update task.
15. Maintenance triggers: reorg (HRIS), naya system/vendor (CMDB/Procurement), contract end, aur office change. Inme se kuch bhi aaye to us plan ka review task banta hai. Review overdue ya contacts purane (config din) to plan "stale".
16. Live disruption mein plan activate hota hai, mass-notification integration se crisis team ko alert, aur decisions timeline mein log. Close hone par PIR zaruri, aur PIR se plan update.
17. 11.4.5 sharing: koi risk ya incident ka learning "share" karne par audience (function/country/role) ko notification aur acknowledgement. Ye Module 1 Tab 4 ke knowledge hub se bhi link hai.
18. Resilience scorecard auto: services within tolerance, tests passed, plans current, SPOF count, aur Module 1 board pack mein feed.
19. `audit_log` har action pe.
