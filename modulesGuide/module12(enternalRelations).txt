Haan bhai, dunga. Pehle research karta hu, phir poori guide.# Research se kya nikla

- **IR (Investor Relations):** Modern IR platforms investor/analyst contacts ka CRM, earnings call aur webcast hosting, aur shareholder ownership tracking ek jagah dete hain. Compliance side pe Reg FD (US), MAR (EU), safe-harbor language, aur earnings materials ke embargo/blackout windows set hote hain.
- **Lobbying aur Government:** US mein quarterly LD-2 reports aur semiannual LD-203 contributions reports lagte hain, aur time tracking mein lobbying aur non-lobbying kaam alag dikhna chahiye. Iske saath FARA, gift aur ethics rules ka integrated framework chahiye.
- **Legal Ops:** Platforms e-billing, matter management, intake/triage aur workflow automation dete hain. LEDES files UTBMS codes ke saath phase/task wise classification karti hain aur invoice auto-validate hota hai. Trend ye hai ki matter management, spend, CLM aur e-billing ek lifecycle (request → contract → dispute → invoice) mein jud rahe hain.
- **IP:** Enterprise IP tools patent/trademark docketing, renewals/annuities, outside counsel coordination aur audit-ready records ke saath aate hain, aur invention intake se prosecution, maintenance aur enforcement tak poora lifecycle cover karte hain.

Structure wahi hai jo 11.0 wali guide ka tha (Common Foundation, Modules, Frontend tabs, Tables, APIs, Core Logic). **Total 6 Screens / 25 Tabs**, aur chart ke saare 42 leaf items covered hain (last mein coverage matrix).

# COMMON FOUNDATION (sab External Relationship modules pe lagu)

**Common rules:** API prefix `/api/ext/...`. Har create/edit/action `audit_log` mein jayega. Har master `effective_from/effective_to` aur `status[draft/active/disabled]` ke saath hoga (delete nahi, disable). Har table mein `entity_id` hoga (multi-company). Policy, threshold, SLA aur country rules **config tables** mein rahenge, hardcode nahi.

**Reuse hone wali tables (pichli guides se):** `audit_log`, `approval_workflows`, `approval_requests`, `approval_actions`, `approval_delegations`, `notifications`, `documents`, `comments`, `custom_fields`, `custom_objects`, `legal_entities`, `countries`, `country_config`, `currencies`, `exchange_rates`, `translations`, `fiscal_calendars`, `org_units`, `locations`, `reason_codes`, aur GRC foundation ki `grc_taxonomy`, `grc_links`, `grc_issues`, `grc_actions`, `grc_evidence`, `regulators`, `third_parties`, `controls`, `obligations`, `attestations`, `gifts_hospitality`.

**(NEW) Common rules add:**
- **Communication governance:** Bahar ki har communication (investor, media, government, regulator) sirf `authorized_spokespersons` se. Unauthorized person ka outbound message ya meeting log hone par flag.
- **Selective disclosure guard:** Material/price-sensitive info kisi ek party ko pehle nahi. Earnings se pehle quiet period/blackout `trading_windows` se auto-lagta hai, aur country ke `disclosure_rules` (Reg FD/MAR/local listing rules) ke hisaab se checks chalte hain.
- **Anti-bribery guard:** Government official (`is_govt_official`) ke saath gift, hospitality, travel ya donation ka pre-approval zaruri, aur limit country config se.
- **Privilege:** `privileged` interaction/document sirf Legal ko dikhta hai, export/print block, aur har access `audit_log` mein.
- **Single interaction log:** Kisi bhi bahari party ke saath har baat-cheet `stakeholder_interactions` mein; commitments auto alag track hote hain.
- **Maker-checker:** Press release, filing, board pack, lobbying report aur settlement jaisi cheezein banane wala khud approve nahi karega.
- **Retention aur legal hold:** Retention policy config se, par legal hold lagne par delete/archive block.
- **Contact data privacy:** External contacts ka consent aur data-retention config, aur data-subject request (access/delete) support.
- **Multi-language aur multi-country:** Sab external-facing content `translations` se, aur filing/threshold rules country config se.

**Dependencies (naam se):** Ledger/Finance, Treasury, Approval Engine, Notification Engine, Document Store, Procurement/AP, HRIS, GRC modules (11.0), Contract/Vendor Master, Analytics, E-signature, Email/Calendar, Media Monitoring feed, Wire/Distribution services, Exchange Filing system.

### Core Logic
1. Covenant actual value Ledger/Treasury se auto-pull hoti hai (formula_json se). Headroom = (threshold - actual) / threshold. Headroom config % se kam ho to early-warning CFO/Treasurer ko, aur breach par turant escalation aur lender notification workflow.
2. Compliance certificate ka preparer aur reviewer alag hote hain. Due date se pehle reminders, aur submission ka lender acknowledgement save hota hai.
3. Maturity profile (debt maturity wall) facilities se auto-banta hai, aur maturity se pehle refinancing task (config months).
4. Rating trigger (e.g. covenant breach par rating action) `credit_ratings.triggers_json` se monitor hota hai.
5. Earnings event banne par `trading_windows` mein quiet period auto-add hota hai. Is dauran analyst/investor meeting log karne ya schedule karne par warning ya block (config).
6. Earnings script, Q&A bank aur press release Disclosure Committee review se guzarte hain. Review ke bina "approved" nahi, aur approved version ka hash lock hota hai.
7. Analyst/investor ko material info sirf public disclosure ke baad. Meeting notes mein "material info shared" flag aaye to Legal ko turant alert (selective disclosure check, country `disclosure_rules` se).
8. Consensus vs guidance mein drift config % se zyada ho to IR + CFO ko alert.
9. Shareholder snapshots source file import se aate hain, aur change from previous snapshot se buyers/sellers dikhte hain.
10. AGM/EGM: notice period (country config) se pehle notice na gaya to red flag. Resolutions ka result record date ke holdings se match hota hai, aur voting results exchange filing task trigger karte hain.
11. Corporate action announce hone par record date, payment date aur filing tasks auto-bante hain. Unclaimed dividend transfer deadline se pehle reminder.
12. Shareholder query ka SLA hota hai (category wise), overdue par escalation, aur regulator-visible grievance report auto-banti hai (country config).
13. Board pack generate hote waqt data ka snapshot lock ho jata hai. Financial results Ledger se pull hoti hain aur CFO commentary ke bina publish nahi. Publish se pehle CFO approval.
14. Audit committee pack ke items Internal Audit (GRC 11.0 Module 3) aur External Audit observations se auto-aate hain, naye duplicate entry ke bina. Overdue findings alag highlight.
15. Independent director ki independence status criteria (Module 3 governance policy) se re-check hoti hai, aur term/independence limit cross hone par alert.
16. Circular resolution ke liye sab directors ki e-signature chahiye, aur quorum/majority rules config se.
17. Board actions agle meeting ke pack mein auto carry-forward hote hain jab tak close na hon.
18. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic
1. Relationship health score = weighted (contact recency, interaction quality, open-issue ageing, commitments kept, sentiment). Weights config se. Score threshold se neeche aaye to owner ko alert.
2. Har govt body/agency ka ek hi `relationship_owner` + backup hota hai (effective-dated). Owner ke bina body "unassigned" flag, aur owner company chhode to turant reassign task.
3. Sirf `authorized_spokespersons` hi govt/agency ke saath formal meeting ya submission log kar sakte hain.
4. Internal advisor request ka SLA hota hai. Overdue par owner ko escalation, aur saare internal inputs ek consolidated position note mein jaate hain jo approve hone par "company position" bante hain.
5. External advisor engagement se pehle conflict check, aur advisor ke fee/deliverable `legal`/procurement ke through approve.
6. Correspondence due date se pehle reminder, aur regulator/authority ke statutory deadlines (country config) red flag ke saath track hote hain.
7. Govt official ke saath gift/hospitality/travel/donation ki request approval ke bina nahi. Limit exceed ya high-risk country par Compliance + Legal approval, aur sab GRC 11.0 `gifts_hospitality` mein sync.
8. Audit inquiry intake par SLA timer shuru. Har document request ka owner aur deadline hota hai, privilege review (Legal) ke bina response submit nahi, aur approval ke baad hi external bhejna.
9. Same topic par alag-alag agencies ko diye gaye responses ka consistency check chalta hai (key facts/figures compare), aur mismatch par Legal ko flag.
10. Inquiry se agar findings/penalty aaye to GRC 11.0 Module 5 mein remediation case auto-ban sakta hai.
11. Contact documentation: `stakeholder_interactions` se auto-compile hoti hai, aur export agency ya time-period wise mil sakta hai (read-only, `audit_log` ke saath).
12. Industry group membership ka evaluation scorecard config criteria se, aur renewal se 90 din pehle (config) review task, notice date miss na ho isliye alert.
13. Association ki public position company position se mismatch ho to alert aur Government Relations head ko review task (alignment).
14. Dues ka lobbying % auto-lobbying spend mein add hota hai (indirect lobbying) taaki disclosure complete ho.
15. Lobbying: `lobbying_time_entries` mein is_lobbying flag se hi spend aur reportable hours calc hote hain. Registration threshold (e.g. US LDA per-quarter limit, config) cross hone par "registration required" alert.
16. Filings (LD-2/LD-203/EU TR) ke due date calendar se, preparer-reviewer alag, aur filing ke baad acknowledgement save. LD-203 ke contributions `political_contributions` se reconcile hote hain.
17. Political contribution pre-clearance ke bina allowed nahi. Limit/blackout rules country config se, aur covered official ko gift/travel rules ke tahat check.
18. Lobbying positions `policy-alignment review` mein company ke public commitments/ESG policies se compare hoti hain, mismatch par Board oversight report mein flag.
19. Board ko quarterly lobbying spend (direct + indirect) report auto-generate hoti hai.
20. `audit_log` har action pe, aur interactions ka history read-only.

### Core Logic
1. Ethics policy publish ke baad immutable hai, edit par naya version. Publish par audience (employees, directors, contractors, third parties) ko acknowledgement request, aur quiz fail par attest complete nahi.
2. Ethics query ka jawab "FAQ" ban sakta hai (anonymized) aur HR/Legal approve karne ke baad hi publish hota hai.
3. DoA matrix effective-dated hai, aur Approval Engine har approval chain DoA se nikalta hai (amount/type/entity). DoA mein koi gap/overlap ho to validation warning.
4. Related-party transaction audit committee approval ke bina process nahi hota. Arm's-length evidence ke bina approval nahi, aur disclosure deadline (country config) track hoti hai.
5. Governance code map se comply/explain status nikalta hai. "Explain" wale items ka explanation board approve karta hai aur annual report ke liye export hota hai.
6. Statutory registers (directors, shareholders, charges) ka as-of-date view, aur change hone par auto-update task aur regulatory filing (country config) trigger.
7. Insider trading: designated person ka trade pre-clearance tabhi jab `trading_windows` open ho aur person `insider_list` mein na ho (ya approval ke saath). Executed trade aur holdings declaration reconcile hote hain.
8. Preventive program ka audience rule se enrolled log nikalte hain, completion aur quiz score track hota hai. Overdue par manager escalation.
9. Legal risk assessment ka heat rating GRC 11.0 risk register se link hota hai (high rating par risk auto-suggest).
10. Playbook fallback positions contract drafting (Module 4 Tab 5) mein use hote hain, taaki business bina Legal ke standard cases close kare.
11. Effectiveness analytics mein programs ke pehle/baad ke dispute/claim trends compare hote hain, aur low-effectiveness programs par review.
12. Compliance program plan risk-based hoti hai. GRC 11.0 ke risk register aur obligations se priority topics suggest hote hain. Plan approve hone ke baad hi initiatives active.
13. Initiative close karne ke liye evidence zaruri hai (training completion, test result, etc.), aur corrective actions `grc_actions` mein sync.
14. Program effectiveness review annual hota hai (design, resourcing, testing, continuous improvement), aur gaps ka action plan.
15. Investigation case confidential hai. Sirf assigned investigator, Legal aur HR (as needed) ko dikhta hai. Retaliation monitoring: reporter ke baad ke HR actions (performance rating, transfer, termination) par flag.
16. Third-party DD high-risk tier (country, industry, govt-interaction) par enhanced DD, aur DD ke bina contract/payment block (Procurement integration, config).
17. Sanctions screening party onboarding, payment aur periodic re-screen par chalti hai. "Potential match" par payment hold aur Compliance review.
18. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic
1. **Intake triage:** Legal request aane par category/urgency/jurisdiction se routing rule chalta hai. "Outside counsel" decide hone par matter ban jata hai aur `work_requirement_brief` zaruri hota hai (scope, expertise, jurisdiction, exposure, timeline).
2. Outside counsel ka engagement tabhi jab conflict check clear ho, rate card approved ho, engagement letter attach ho aur firm ne billing guidelines accept ki ho. Panel se bahar ki firm ke liye exception approval.
3. Strategy aur budget (phase/task wise, UTBMS) approve hone ke baad hi matter "active". Budget exceed hone ka forecast (spend + forecast to complete) config % se zyada ho to Legal head ko alert, aur scope change request re-approval ke saath.
4. Work product `matter_deliverables` mein privilege tag ke saath aata hai. Status report cadence (config) miss hone par counsel ko reminder, aur milestone ageing escalation.
5. Reserves/provisioning: `probable/possible/remote` assessment se reserve amount tay hota hai, Finance ko monthly accrual file jati hai (accounting-standard rules config se), aur reserve change par CFO/GC approval.
6. **Invoice processing:** LEDES upload par lines parse hote hain, aur rule engine chalta hai (rate cap, block billing, duplicate lines, unauthorized timekeeper, UTBMS code validity, expense policy). Flagged lines auto-adjust ya reviewer ko jate hain, aur adjustment reason counsel ko wapas bheji jati hai.
7. Invoice approval DoA aur matter budget se. Approved invoice AP mein post hota hai (tax/withholding, currency), aur payment status wapas sync. Budget se zyada invoice par approval level badhta hai.
8. Accrual: monthly WIP/accrued legal fees `legal_accruals` se Ledger ko; reconciliation mein mismatch flag.
9. **Performance:** Firm scorecard (cost vs budget, outcome, timeliness, responsiveness, diversity) har matter close par feedback se aur invoice data se auto-banta hai. Annual panel review mein low-score firm par action (rate renegotiation, restricted, exit).
10. **Disputes:** Har dispute ek matter se linked hai. Court/hearing events se deadlines calculate hoti hain (jurisdiction rules config), aur limitation tracker claim ki expiry se pehle alert.
11. Legal hold issue hone par custodians ko notice, acknowledgement tracking, pending par escalation, aur hold ke dauraan retention/delete block (common rule). Release sirf Legal head approval se.
12. Settlement authority DoA matrix se; offer amount authority se upar ho to GC/CFO/Board approval. Settlement payment AP ke through aur reserve release auto-adjust.
13. Dispute outcome/lessons learned se GRC 11.0 root cause aur preventive program (Module 3) mein improvement suggestion.
14. Insurance: dispute claim par applicable policy ka notice window miss na ho (policy condition se due date), aur reimbursement tracking.
15. **Advice:** Request intake form topic se routing (marketing, privacy, HR, M&A) ho kar sahi specialist ko. SLA breach par escalation, similar earlier advice dikhaya jata hai (privileged-aware), aur advice document default privileged.
16. Repeated advice queries (config threshold) se playbook/policy/FAQ update ka suggestion Legal head ko jata hai.
17. **Contracts:** Request template se draft; clause library ke approved clause se. Standard se deviation ka risk score auto-calc hota hai (liability cap, indemnity, termination, governing law), aur high-risk par Legal approval zaruri.
18. Approval chain DoA se; signatory authority (Module 3 registers) check hota hai, aur e-signature ke baad executed copy repository mein lock hoti hai.
19. Obligations (renewal, notice period, payment, SLA, deliverable) metadata aur AI-extraction se banti hain (human confirm), aur due date se pehle owner ko reminder. Auto-renew notice date miss na ho isliye notice-period se pehle alert.
20. Contract ek matter, vendor/customer record, spend aur IP license (Module 5) se linked rehta hai.
21. `audit_log` har action pe, aur privileged documents ka access log alag.

### Core Logic
1. Invention disclosure committee review se file/hold/trade-secret decision hota hai. "File" par `ip_asset` ban jata hai aur docketing start. "Trade secret" par `trade_secret_register` mein entry (details restricted).

3. Har docket item ke teen reminders (config days pehle) aur miss hone ke risk par escalation. Grace period alag track hoti hai, aur "lapsed" status ka alert IP head ko turant.
4. Annuity/renewal ke liye "pay / abandon / review" decision value-based hota hai (business unit usage, licensing income, strategic importance). Abandon decision approval se, aur abandon hone par rights loss ka warning.
5. Ownership change (assignment, merger, name change) `ip_chain_of_title` mein, aur register (patent/trademark office) mein record hui ya nahi track hota hai. Unrecorded transfer par flag.
6. Employee/contractor IP assignment agreement ke bina inventor ko disclosure submit karne par warning (HRIS/Contract link).
7. Encumbrance (lien/security interest) hone par license/assignment/abandon action par warning aur Legal approval.
8. Enforcement case Module 4 ke dispute workflow se link hota hai, aur evidence log, cease-and-desist template, aur outcome se portfolio status update.
9. OSS scan results se license obligations (copyleft vs permissive) nikalte hain, aur incompatible license ke saath product release par flag (Engineering integration).
10. Trade secret register ka access list HR (joiner/mover/leaver) se sync, exit checklist mein access revoke aur NDA reminder.
11. License register ke terms (field, territory, exclusivity, minimum) validation mein use hote hain. Royalty statement aane par calculated royalty tiers se compute hoti hai aur reported se compare, variance config % se zyada ho to "disputed" flag aur audit right ke tahat audit trigger.
12. Royalty payment/receipt AP/AR se, tax withholding (country rules) ke saath, aur Ledger mein post. Minimum guarantee shortfall auto-calc.
13. Change-of-control/assignment par jin licenses mein consent chahiye, wo list auto-nikalti hai (M&A ke samay).
14. License expiry/renewal se 120 din (config) pehle review task, aur renewal na ho to product/revenue impact analysis.
15. Option ka exercise window `option_end` se countdown, 90/60/30 din alerts. Exercise decision valuation worksheet, business case aur approval (DoA) ke baad hi. Lapse hone se pehle explicit "let lapse" decision zaruri (accidental lapse nahi).
16. Option exercise hone par resulting license/asset record auto-draft hota hai (Tab 3/Tab 1) aur contract, payment aur docket tasks banti hain.
17. IP cost tracker invoices (Module 4 e-billing) se auto-sync, aur portfolio cost vs budget alert.
18. `audit_log` har action pe, trade secret records mein field-level masking.

### Core Logic
1. Community program ke outcomes (beneficiaries, spend, social value) period-wise capture hote hain, aur CSR/ESG reports mein auto-pull. Budget Ledger se compare hota hai.
2. Community grievance ka SLA category wise, overdue par site head ko escalation, aur high-severity grievance GRC 11.0 risk/issue mein auto-link. Satisfaction survey close ke baad.
3. Social license index = weighted (engagement frequency, open grievances, sentiment). Index gir kar threshold se neeche aaye to site-level action plan.
4. Media inquiry sirf `authorized_spokespersons` se jawab. Inquiry par deadline timer, aur sensitive topic (price-sensitive, litigation, regulator) mein Legal/IR approval ke bina response nahi.
5. Media contacts consent aur privacy rules ke saath, aur opt-out respect.
6. Media coverage import se sentiment, reach, topic tagging; negative coverage spike par PR head + owner ko alert, aur linked issue/risk auto-suggest.
7. Crisis activation par playbook, holding statement aur approvers load hote hain. Statements ek hi source se jaate hain (consistency), aur timeline log hota hai. GRC 11.0 (business resilience/remediation) ke events se auto-trigger.
8. Social accounts ka owner aur approver, aur official account ke bina koi channel "company voice" nahi.
9. Political/country risk indicators se risk level, aur level badhne par GRC risk register mein auto-risk suggestion aur resilience plan (11.4) review task.
10. Political scenarios mein triggers aur responses defined hote hain (security, staff safety, comms, supply). Trigger fire hone par crisis comm aur HR/Security ko alert.
11. Public position statements approve hone ke baad version-locked, aur country-wise consistency check (ek hi topic par alag-alag countries mein contradiction flag). Position lobbying activities (Module 2) mein use hoti hai.
12. Guardrails: company political neutrality policy, aur koi bhi political activity (contribution, endorsement, event sponsorship) Module 2 ki pre-clearance aur anti-bribery checks se guzarta hai.
13. **Press release create:** Request se draft; fact list (`press_release_facts`) mein har claim ka source aur verifier zaruri, aur quotes speaker approval ke bina allowed nahi.
14. Review: Legal sab releases ke liye. `price_sensitive` flag par Disclosure Committee/IR review zaruri aur exchange filing sync. Executive approval ke baad version hash lock, aur edit par re-approval.
15. **Issue:** Release calendar se conflict check (earnings quiet period, anya announcement, holiday), embargo time ka enforcement, aur multi-language versions simultaneous.
16. Price-sensitive release pehle exchange filing, phir media/wire (ya simultaneously, country rules config). Issue ke baad selective disclosure check ke liye audit trail.
17. Distribution multi-channel (wire, email, web, social, internal), delivery/open tracking, aur correction/retraction ka alag approval workflow aur public notice.
18. Newsroom archive retention aur takedown rules config se, legal hold lagne par takedown/delete block.
19. Pickup analytics release-wise coverage, reach aur sentiment ko link karti hai, aur quarterly PR report mein.
20. `audit_log` har action pe.
