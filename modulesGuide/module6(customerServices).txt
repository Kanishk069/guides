**Common rules (platform level):**
- **Unified customer timeline:** har channel ki interaction, case, order, warranty, service, complaint ek customer record pe dikhta hai. Reports FCR ko channel-wise nahi, customer-level pe naapte hain.
- **Omnichannel routing engine:** skill + capacity + priority + SLA + customer tier + language, ye sab routing ke inputs hain.
- **SLA engine:** timers business hours/holiday calendar ke hisaab se chalte hain. Pause/resume (customer reply pending), breach warning aur auto-escalation.
- **Entitlement engine:** customer ya asset ko kya support milega (contract, warranty, tier, prepaid hours) wo har case pe auto-check hota hai.
- **Approval Engine:** parallel steps, conditional steps, send-back, delegation, escalation on SLA breach, fallback approver.
- **Consent & privacy:** recording, marketing aur profiling ke liye alag consent. Country rule se recording prompt aur PCI redaction auto-apply hota hai. Right-to-erasure se customer data anonymize hota hai, par regulatory file hold rehti hai.
- **AI layer (har module mein):** agent assist, case summary, sentiment, auto-classification, auto-QA, suggested reply. Har AI output "suggested" rehta hai aur human override logged hota hai.
- **Multi-country / multi-language / multi-currency:** local law, local SLA, local template.
- **Event bus:** har state change ek event hai (`case.created`, `claim.approved`, `recall.initiated`), jisse integrations aur analytics chalte hain.

**Dependencies (naam se):** CRM, ERP (orders, inventory, finance), PLM/Quality (CAPA), Supplier Portal, Telephony/CCaaS, Payment Gateway, Logistics/Reverse Logistics, Marketing, Sales/Opportunity, HR (agent master), Data Warehouse/Analytics, Identity.

### Core Logic
1. Required FTE = workload / (occupancy cap) with shrinkage uplift. Industry mein shrinkage kareeb 30-35% maani jati hai, isliye isko hamesha alag config se add karo, kabhi ignore mat karo. Default value tenant/site wise config.
2. Erlang C ek baseline hai. Ye 1917 mein phone trunks ke liye bana tha, isliye predictable patterns par 8-15% overstaff aur peaks par understaff kar sakta hai. Isliye default model ML forecast rakho, Erlang sirf fallback/benchmark, aur accuracy (WAPE) har run ke baad store karo.
3. Voice, chat aur email ke liye alag staffing math (chat mein concurrency, email/social mein backlog-based).
4. Schedule publish hone par agents ko notification, aur Labor rule violation par publish block.
5. Real-time adherence agent state (telephony event) vs schedule se calc. Threshold breach par supervisor alert.
6. Intraday: actual volume forecast se config % se zyada hile to reforecast, aur suggested actions (overtime/VTO/skill reroute).
7. Manual QA mein aksar 1% se kam volume evaluate hota hai, isliye auto-QA 100% interactions score kare aur human review sirf low-score, dispute, compliance-fail aur calibration sample par ho.
8. Auto-fail item (jaise recording disclosure miss, card number read-back) score ko override karke compliance alert banata hai.
9. Auto-QA score hamesha "suggested" rehta hai. Agent dispute kar sakta hai, aur human decision final.
10. Coaching plan low-scoring behavior se auto-suggest hota hai aur follow-up evaluation se improvement measure hota hai.
11. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic
1. Omnichannel basics jo har enterprise platform mein non-negotiable maane jate hain: ek unified customer timeline, shared queue jo sab channels ko route kare, maintained knowledge base, channel-wise SLA tracking, aur FCR ko customer level pe measure karna. Ye saari cheezein is screen ki foundation hain.
2. Case create hote hi identity resolution chalta hai. Match nahi mila to naya customer (duplicate detection ke saath), match mila to timeline attach.
3. Entitlement check: entitlement define karta hai ki customer ko contract, prepaid hours ya tier ke hisaab se kis level ka support milega. Case ka SLA entitlement se aata hai, aur out-of-entitlement par paid service/quote ka option.
4. SLA timer business hours calendar se chalta hai. "Pending customer" par pause, customer reply par auto-resume. Breach se pehle warning (config %), breach par escalation.

11. Upsell: consent aur case-state rule (open complaint/negative sentiment par offer block) pass hone par hi opportunity capture, aur CRM mein push. Won/lost reason wapas aata hai.

GDPR ke tahat recording ke liye explicit consent ya legitimate interest chahiye, aur customer kabhi bhi consent wapas le sakta hai. Payment card data aaye to pause-and-resume ya post-call redaction.

### Core Logic
1. Return eligibility: window + category rule + condition + serial validation, sab config. Fail hone par reject with reason, ya exception approval.
2. Serial/customer-level abuse score (bar-bar returns, same serial dobara) high ho to manual review.
3. Receiving par serial match fail ho (return swap) to quarantine, fraud case aur refund hold.

6. **Regulatory clocks (config-driven, seeded):**
   - US consumer products (CPSC Section 15(b)): firms ko "immediately" yani 24 ghante ke andar report karna hota hai jab tak reasonably supporting information mile.
   - US medical devices (FDA MDR 21 CFR 803): awareness ke 30 calendar days ke andar, aur remedial action ya FDA ke request par 5 work days. Dhyan rahe ki 30 din calendar days hain aur 5 din work days, aur unit galat karna aam galti hai.

   - EU consumer products (GPSR): accident hone par manufacturer ko turant Safety Business Gateway se member state authority ko notify karna hota hai. Importer/distributor manufacturer ko inform karte hain.
   - India e-commerce complaint timelines: Screen 4 Logic 1.
   Ye sab tables `regulatory_clock_rules` mein jayenge, lawyer/regulatory affairs se review ke baad hi production mein, aur country-wise override ho sakta hai. Regulations badalte rehte hain, isliye ye "defaults" hain, final nahi.

8. Deadline se pehle countdown alert (config % ya hours), aur miss hone ka risk dikhe to Compliance head ko escalation.
9. Report mein multiple regimes ek incident par lag sakte hain (jaise ek hi device event pe FDA aur EU dono), aur system har regime ka alag report/clock banata hai.
10. Regulator ko submitted data ka snapshot immutable, aur follow-up report naya version.
11. `audit_log` har action pe, aur regulatory records ki retention legal minimum ke saath alag policy.

### Core Logic
1. Recall signal scanner: complaint spikes (Screen 4), warranty failure clusters by lot/part (Screen 7), return-reason clusters (Screen 5), safety incidents (Screen 5) aur supplier alerts ko continuously scan karke "potential recall" suggest karta hai. Suggest hone ka matlab recall nahi, committee review shuru hota hai.
2. Scope builder: lot/serial/date range se `assets`, `product_registrations`, orders aur dealer shipments join karke affected units, customers aur channels nikalta hai. Jo units ka customer unknown hai unko "unidentifiable (public notice only)" mark.
3. Containment (stop-sale/ship-hold) recall approval ke saath hi turant execute ho sakta hai (committee approval se pehle emergency containment allowed, alag approval).
4. Hazard assessment mein likelihood aur severity se risk score banta hai, aur classification regime ke hisaab se (class I/II/III ya equivalent) config scale se. Reassessment version history ke saath.
5. Regulatory trigger logic: US consumer products mein 24 ghante ka "immediately" clock aur recall ke baad CPSC ko monthly progress reports, medical devices mein 21 CFR 806 ke 10 working days (Screen 5 Logic 6), EU mein GPSR. GPSR recall mein affected consumers ko effective, cost-free aur timely remedy ka haq hai, aur kam se kam do options (repair, replacement ya refund) offer karne padte hain, isliye `recall_remedies` mein EU scope ke liye kam se kam do options ki validation. Recall notice ke liye EU mein Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1435 ka mandatory template, aur direct notification ka rule: responsible economic operator ko affected consumers ko directly, simple language mein, turant writing mein inform karna hota hai. Ye sab config + country template se, hardcode nahi.
6. Communication wave plan: registered customers ko direct, baaki ko public notice. Non-responders ko config gap (jaise 14/30/60 din) par reminder wave. Har send ka delivery/bounce track.
7. Public serial lookup page: serial/lot daalo to affected/not affected aur remedy options, aur kisi bhi customer ki personal data expose na ho.
8. Remedy requests recall response se `warranty_claims` (campaign type) ya `service_orders` ya `return_requests` create karte hain, aur status recall scope unit se sync.
9. Effectiveness checks: FDA-style effectiveness check levels (A se F) audit kiye jane wale consignees ke percentage se juday hote hain. System check level config mein rakhta hai aur sample size recall class se suggest karta hai.
10. Inventory reconciliation: expected vs found vs quarantined vs reworked vs scrapped. Gap par investigation task.
11. Termination criteria (response threshold, remedy completion, regulator concurrence) config se. Sab satisfy hone par hi "termination requested", aur regulator approval ke baad terminated. Terminate ke baad data retention lock aur monitoring window.
12. Cost tracking aur lessons learned (CAPA/design change) product/quality teams ko feed.
13. Recall data legal hold: edit/delete band, sirf append-only versions.
14. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic

2. Metrics ka matlab alag hai, isliye teeno rakho: NPS loyalty/recommendation, CSAT satisfaction, aur CES yeh dikhata hai ki issue solve karwana kitna aasan tha. 7. Insight route: topic/product ke hisaab se product-design, marketing, manufacturing team ko auto-route, aur acknowledgement + status zaruri. Unacknowledged insights par reminder.

11. Survey consent: marketing-type survey ke liye alag consent. Service-satisfaction survey legitimate purpose, aur opt-out har invitation mein.

### Core Logic
1. Warranty metrics `warranty_claims`, `assets`, ERP sales aur recovery data se auto-compute. Claim rate = claims / units in-warranty-exposure (units sold nahi, exposure window wale), warna rate galat aayega.
2. Accrual vs actual variance se warranty reserve model calibrate hota hai, aur finance ko report.
3. Waste scan rules: NFF rate (part replace hua par defect nahi mila), same part ek asset pe bar-bar, rates schedule se zyada, duplicate payment, supplier-responsible par recovery na hona, policy se bahar goodwill. Har finding ka rupee impact estimate.
4. Fraud case workbench mein signals (Screen 7 Logic 2) drill-down ke saath, aur dealer-level analysis. Detection ke baad outcome (confirmed/cleared) model feedback mein jata hai taaki false positives kam hon.
5. Fraud action ke liye due process: kisi customer/dealer ko block/chargeback karne se pehle investigation, evidence aur approval. Sirf score ke aadhar par adverse action nahi.
6. Dealer overpayment recovery chargeback ke roop mein, aur watchlist ka periodic review.
7. Warranty satisfaction Screen 11 ke survey data se, aur time-to-resolution vs CSAT correlation.
8. Recall scorecard recall events se auto-compute, aur detection latency = first signal date se recall decision date. Missed signals review se signal scanner thresholds tune hote hain.
9. Mock recall drill: ek lot/serial range choose karke dekho kitne affected customers/dealers/inventory kitne time mein identify ho paye. Isse traceability gaps (jaise unregistered units) dikhte hain.
10. Dashboards ke thresholds `cs_thresholds` se (RAG).
11. `audit_log` har action pe.
