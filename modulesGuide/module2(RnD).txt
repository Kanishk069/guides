# RESEARCH NOTES (guide mein jo cheezein inse aayi)

**Regulatory (sab dates config tables mein rakhna, hardcode nahi, aur legal team se verify karwana):**
- Cyber Resilience Act ke reporting duties 11 Sept 2026 se shuru hain: manufacturers ko actively exploited vulnerabilities aur serious cyber incidents report karne hain. Naya Product Liability Directive 9 Dec 2026 ke baad market mein aane wale products par lagu hoga aur software, AI aur cybersecurity defects ko bhi cover karega.
- ESPR ka framework aur EU Central DPP Registry 19 July 2026 se live hone wala tha, lekin product-wise delegated acts abhi aane baaki hain, isliye batteries ke baad ki sab dates indicative hain. DPP ke access levels alag hote hain (public vs restricted), aur operators ke paas DPP continuity ka contingency plan hona chahiye. Data carrier QR ya barcode ho sakta hai, aur ek single central database banana maqsad nahi hai.
- GPSR mein non-EU manufacturer ko EU-based responsible person naam-zad karna padta hai, aur products par type/batch/serial number hona chahiye.
- FDA ka QMSR ISO 13485 ke saath align hua hai (Feb 2026), isliye medical device wale clients ke liye design controls aur documentation zaruri hain.

**Recall:**
- US mein NHTSA ko defect determine hone ke 5 business days ke andar report karna hota hai. NHTSA quarterly recall reports maangta hai, aur FDA har 2-4 hafte mein status report maang sakta hai.
- CPSC ke Fast Track mein report ke 20 working days ke andar voluntary recall implement karna hota hai. FDA effectiveness checks ke levels A se F tak hote hain, aur NHTSA mein registered owners ko 60 din ke andar notice jaata hai.
- EU Safety Gate alert corrective measure ke 4 working days ke andar jaata hai. Ek guide ke hisaab se lot/serial traceability kam se kam 10 saal retain honi chahiye, aur hotline se quality aur regulatory tak escalation clocks (2h / 4h / 24h) predefined hone chahiye. CPSC compliance records kam se kam 5 saal rakhne ko kehta hai.

**Warranty:**
- Warranty reserve = units sold x expected claim rate x average cost per claim. US GAAP mein assurance-type warranty ASC 460 ke tahat accrue hoti hai jab cost probable aur estimable ho. Assurance-type aur service-type warranty ka classification (ASC 606 / IFRS 15) accounting treatment decide karta hai, aur extended warranty aksar service-type hoti hai.
- Claim rate cohort ke hisaab se nikalo (us cohort ke units sold se), us mahine ki sales se nahi. Supplier recovery rate = accepted recovery / requested recovery.
- Fraud types: non-covered events, inflated repair cost, duplicate claims, ineligible products. Rule-based flags (cost threshold, coverage window, high-frequency claimant) ke saath AI pattern detection bhi chahiye.

**PLM / Portfolio / IP:**
- PLM ka digital thread as-designed, as-manufactured aur as-maintained ke beech traceability deta hai. Product-in-service (IoT) data wapas PLM mein feed hona chahiye taaki closed-loop improvement ho.
- Enterprise buyers ko configurable stage-gates (approval quorum logic ke saath), audit trails, role-based access, aur ERP/PLM/Jira/BI integration chahiye.
- Ek chhooti hui patent annuity se granted patent lapse ho sakta hai. Docketing system jurisdiction ke hisaab se deadlines khud calculate karta hai. Open-source license scanning (FOSSology jaise tools) software products mein IP obligations pakadne ke liye zaruri hai.

**Common rules (advanced):**
- **Released-object rule:** released BOM, spec, drawing, requirement baseline, policy, technical file direct edit nahi hota. Change sirf Change Engine (ECR/ECO) se, effectivity ke saath.
- **Stage-Gate Engine:** gates config se, criteria + evidence + quorum approval. Koi bhi project gate pass kiye bina aage nahi badhta.
- **Digital thread:** requirement ↔ design ↔ BOM ↔ test ↔ regulation ↔ warranty/field data sab `trace_links` se. Upstream object badalne par downstream links `suspect_flag` hote hain.
- **Three product views:** as-designed (EBOM), as-built (MBOM + lot/serial genealogy), as-maintained (service BOM + installed base).
- **Effective-dating aur as-of reports:** koi bhi report kisi bhi date ke hisaab se chalegi.
- **Classification-based access:** IP-restricted aur export-controlled data role + country + purpose se gated, har access `audit_log` mein.
- **Multi-country / currency / language:** amounts ke saath currency, regulatory data country-wise, user-facing text `translations` se.
- **Integrations bidirectional:** ERP, CAD, MES, QMS, CRM, Jira/Azure DevOps, LMS. Har synced record ka `external_refs` mein mapping.
- **Approval engine:** parallel steps, quorum, send-back, delegation, SLA escalation, fallback approver.

**Dependencies (naam se):** Approval Engine, Notification Engine, Document Store, ERP/Ledger, Procurement/Supplier Management, QMS, MES/Manufacturing, CRM/Service, Learning (LMS), HR (employees, skills, capacity), Legal/Contracts, Analytics.

### Core Logic
1. Portfolio snapshot nightly ERP/CRM/Analytics se aata hai, `health_score` = `health_score_rules` ke weighted metrics.
2. Underperformer rule: health score threshold se N periods neeche to automatic review task.
3. Priority score = sum(weight x normalized score), models versioned. Optimizer budget + capacity + risk caps ke andar best NPV mix nikalta hai.
4. Funded/deferred/killed decision mein rationale mandatory, quorum approval Gate Engine se.
5. Funded item par automatically project (Module 7) banta hai aur target set baseline lock hota hai. Target badalna = naya version + reason.
6. Allowable cost = target price x (1 - target margin). Cost targets ka sum allowable cost se zyada ho to red flag.
7. Actual cost/quality/timing Module 7, Module 2 aur ERP se aate hain, variance par alert.
8. Modification impact analysis `trace_links` aur where-used se auto, approved hone par Change Engine mein ECR.
9. Multi-currency: group base currency mein budget rate se conversion.
10. `audit_log` har action pe.

### Core Logic

4. Design spec har requirement se trace, requirement coverage < threshold to gate fail.

11. Production readiness: mandatory items complete + validation runs passed (IQ/OQ/PQ, Cpk threshold) hone par hi launch plan (Module 2) readiness green. Initial run monitor ke defect threshold cross par auto-alert aur hold.
12. Launch procedures validated hone tak production release block.

### FRONTEND: Screen 8: Warranty and Recall Management (5 tabs)

**Tab 1. Warranty Policies** (2.3.4.1)

**Tab 3. Supplier Warranty** (2.3.4.3)

**Tab 4. Customer Offerings & Communication** (2.3.4.4, 2.3.4.5)

**Tab 5. Recall Strategy** (2.3.5)

### Core Logic
1. Warranty policy ka accounting_type finance ko jata hai: assurance-type to reserve/accrual, service-type to separate performance obligation (deferred revenue). Reserve = units sold x expected claim rate x avg cost per claim, claim rate cohort-wise.
2. Policy country ke statutory floor se kam nahi ho sakti (higher-of rule). Naye policy version se purane serials par purani terms apply (serial ke sale date ke hisaab se).
3. Claim intake par coverage check: serial + purchase date/registration + usage, claim codes mandatory, labour standard se compare.
4. Auto-approve sirf limit ke andar aur fraud score low hone par. Fraud rules (duplicate, out-of-window, high-frequency claimant, cost outliers) + anomaly model, flag par manual review.
5. Supplier recovery: component-level liability % aur failure thresholds se auto-eligibility. Recovery rate = accepted / requested. Dispute par RCA request, scorecard Module 4/Procurement ko feed.
6. Claim clusters (same defect code/lot) threshold cross karein to early-warning alert, improvement item (Module 5), problem report (Module 7) aur recall evaluation trigger.
7. Recall decision framework (severity x probability x exposure) se recommended action, final decision quorum approval ke saath. Decision ka reasoning documented.
8. Recall case open hote hi traceability (Module 4) se affected lots/serials aur customers ka forward trace, aur lots `status = recalled/blocked`, ERP mein stop-ship.
9. Regulator notifications `notification_matrix` + `regulatory_clock_rules` (Module 3) se auto-schedule, clock breach par exec escalation. Progress/status reports periodic due dates ke saath.
10. Effectiveness checks (response %, correction %) target se neeche to additional outreach action auto-create.
11. Mock recall exercises: time-to-trace KPI, gaps closure tasks. Recall records retention Module 3 retention rules se.
12. Recall/warranty costs Ledger ko, aur reserve adjustments Finance ko.
13. `audit_log` har action pe.
