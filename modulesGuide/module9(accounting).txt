## BACKEND — API Endpoints
```

POST /api/revenue/obligations/:id/recognize          → post recognition for a period (creates journal entry)

GET  /api/revenue/deferred-revenue                   → deferred revenue tracker
```

## BACKEND — Core Logic
1. **5-step model** (ASC 606/IFRS 15 standard): (1) Identify contract → (2) Identify distinct performance obligations → (3) Determine transaction price → (4) Allocate transaction price to obligations → (5) Recognize revenue when/as obligation satisfied
2. Point-in-time obligations recognize 100% on delivery/completion; over-time (e.g. SaaS subscription) recognize straight-line across period; percentage-completion (e.g. construction/IT projects) recognize proportional to % complete input
3. AR Invoice (billing) and Revenue Recognition are **decoupled** — billing an invoice doesn't automatically mean revenue is earned; deferred_revenue = billed - recognized
4. "Post Recognition" creates Journal Entry: Debit Deferred Revenue (Liability), Credit Revenue (Income) — only for the recognized portion
5. Contract modification triggers "Recalculate Allocation" — re-splits remaining transaction price across remaining obligations
6. Har contract/obligation/recognition action `audit_log` mein record hoga

# 30. Global Trade Services (9.11.1 – 9.11.10)

## FRONTEND, 6 Tabs

**Tab 1: Sanctioned Party Screening** (9.11.1)
- Auto screening of customers, vendors, banks, shipping parties against sanctions lists (OFAC, UN, EU, MHA/local).
- Screening result: Clear / Potential Match / Confirmed Match, reviewer decision, reason.
- Re-screening schedule when lists update.

**Tab 2: Export/Import Control & Product Classification** (9.11.2, 9.11.3)
- Product classification: item, HS code, export control class, license required (Y/N).
- Trade controls: license register (number, validity, quantity/value used).
- Order block if license missing or the destination is restricted.

**Tab 3: Duty & Currency** (9.11.5, 9.11.4)
- Duty calculator: HS code, origin, CIF value, BCD, IGST, cess, anti-dumping, preference applied.
- Currency conversion: customs exchange rate (notified) vs transaction rate.

**Tab 4: Customs Communication & Trade Documents** (9.11.6, 9.11.7)
- Customs filings: Bill of Entry / Shipping Bill, status, customs reference, queries.
- Documents: invoice, packing list, BL/AWB, certificate of origin, insurance. Upload, status, completeness check.

**Tab 5: Trade Preferences & Restitution** (9.11.8, 9.11.9)
- Preferences: FTA/origin eligibility, certificate of origin, duty saved.
- Restitution: duty drawback/refund claims (RoDTEP, drawback), claim status, amount received.

**Tab 6: Letters of Credit** (9.11.10)
- "+ New LC": applicant/beneficiary, issuing/advising bank, amount, currency, expiry, terms, documents required.
- Tracking: issued, amended, documents presented, discrepancy, paid/expired.

## CORE LOGIC

2. Sales order/shipment pe classification + license check, bina valid license ke block.
3. Duty = assessable value (customs rate se INR) × duty rates. Landed cost Inventory mein add hota hai (Module 34).
4. Customs rate Multi-Currency (33) ke normal rate se alag store hoti hai.
5. LC payment/receipt Bank module aur AR/AP se link hota hai, discrepancy pe payment hold.
6. Restitution claim milne par JE (Duty Drawback Income / Receivable vs Bank).
7. Documents incomplete ho toh shipment filing block.
